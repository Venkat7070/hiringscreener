# Android Phones Product Line

ReNew Mobile carries a broad range of refurbished Android phones, primarily from Samsung, Google, and OnePlus, along with select other major manufacturers depending on available supply. This article covers what's typically in our catalog and Android-specific questions our support team frequently receives.

## Brands and Models Typically Available

- **Samsung Galaxy S and Note series** — flagship models, typically the most requested Android line in our catalog
- **Samsung Galaxy A series** — mid-range models offering strong value
- **Google Pixel series** — known for close-to-stock Android software and strong camera performance
- **OnePlus** — flagship and mid-range models, when supply is available

As with our iPhone inventory, exact model, storage, color, and grade availability changes frequently based on incoming trade-in and buyback supply. Each product listing reflects real-time availability for that specific configuration.

## Storage and Expandable Storage

Android phone storage capacity is listed the same way as iPhones — as a fixed amount (such as 128GB or 256GB) set at time of manufacture. Some Android models, particularly certain Samsung Galaxy A series devices, include a microSD card slot for expandable storage; this is noted on the product listing when applicable, since it's not a universal feature across all Android phones. Google Pixel and most Samsung flagship models generally do not include expandable storage.

## Carrier Status and Unlocking

The same Unlocked vs. Carrier-locked distinction described in our iPhone article applies to Android devices. Most of our Android inventory is unlocked and compatible with any carrier supporting the device's cellular bands. Listings clearly state carrier status, and customers needing broad carrier compatibility (for switching providers or international use) should choose Unlocked listings.

## Google Account Lock (Factory Reset Protection)

Android devices tied to a Google account are protected by Factory Reset Protection (FRP), which is Android's equivalent to iPhone's Activation Lock — it prevents a device from being set up by anyone other than the account it's registered to until that lock is removed. As with Activation Lock on iPhones, confirming full removal of FRP is a mandatory step in our refurbishment process before any Android device is listed for sale. If a customer ever encounters an FRP screen asking for a previous owner's Google account during setup, this should be reported to support immediately for an immediate replacement or refund at no cost.

Samsung devices specifically also have Samsung's own Reactivation Lock, tied to a Samsung account rather than (or in addition to) a Google account — this is checked and removed separately during our refurbishment process for Samsung devices.

## Battery Health on Android Devices

Every Android phone we sell has a battery health of at least 85% of original design capacity at time of sale, tested using manufacturer or third-party diagnostic tools during our refurbishment process. Unlike iPhones, most Android manufacturers don't include a simple built-in battery health percentage in system settings the way Apple does — Samsung and Google Pixel devices vary in what battery information is exposed to the user directly on the device. Our product listings state the tested battery health percentage at time of sale directly, so customers don't need to rely on an on-device reading to know this figure.

## Android Version and Software Updates

Android phones ship with the most current Android OS version and security patch level available and compatible with that specific model at the time of refurbishment. How long a given model continues receiving OS version upgrades and security patches from its manufacturer varies significantly by brand and model — Google Pixel and Samsung Galaxy flagship models generally receive the longest official update support windows among Android manufacturers, while older or budget models may have already reached the end of their manufacturer update window. This information isn't something we track or guarantee ourselves, since it's determined by the device manufacturer, not by ReNew Mobile — customers wanting long-term software support should research the specific model's manufacturer update policy before purchasing if this is an important factor.

## Fingerprint and Face Unlock

Android devices generally include an in-display or side-mounted fingerprint sensor, and many also support face unlock through the front camera. Both are tested for functionality during refurbishment. As with iPhones, no biometric data from a previous owner is ever preserved on the device — customers enroll their own fingerprint and/or face data during initial setup.

## Dual SIM Support

Many Android models, particularly Samsung's international variants, support dual SIM functionality (either two physical SIM slots, or one physical SIM plus eSIM). Whether a specific unit supports dual SIM, and in what configuration, is listed on the product page — this varies by model and by which regional variant of the device was originally manufactured for, so customers who specifically need dual SIM support should verify this on the listing before ordering.

## Included Accessories

Refurbished Android phones from ReNew Mobile typically ship with a charging cable matching that model's port (almost universally USB-C on recent Android devices), but generally do not include a wall power adapter, consistent with how most manufacturers have sold new Android phones in recent years. A wall adapter can be added from our accessories catalog at checkout if needed.

## Common Compatibility Questions

**Will my existing Android accessories work with a refurbished Android phone?**
This depends on the specific accessory and connector. Nearly all recent Android phones use USB-C, so USB-C cables and chargers are broadly compatible across brands and models, but always check the specific listing for the exact port used, especially for older models.

**Can I transfer data from my old Android phone (or from an iPhone) to a refurbished Android phone?**
Yes — Android supports several data transfer methods, including Google's own device-to-device transfer tools during setup, restoring from a Google account backup, or manufacturer-specific transfer tools (such as Samsung Smart Switch for Samsung devices), which also support transferring from an iPhone in many cases.

**Will a refurbished Samsung phone still have Samsung-specific features like DeX or S Pen support?**
Yes, all software features tied to the specific model (such as Samsung DeX desktop mode, or S Pen support on Note and Ultra series devices) function the same as they would on a new device of that model, since these are software features of that model generally, not something affected by refurbishment. If a listing is for an S Pen-compatible model, note that the S Pen stylus itself is only included if explicitly stated on the product listing — it is not automatically included with every S Pen-compatible device unless listed as in-box.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do refurbished Android phones come with a manufacturer warranty from Samsung or Google?**
A: No — manufacturer warranties are tied to the original purchaser and point of sale and do not transfer to secondhand devices. All ReNew Mobile Android phones are instead covered under our own 12-month warranty described in the Warranty Policy article.

**Q: Can I install a custom ROM or root a refurbished Android phone?**
A: Customers are free to modify their device however they choose after purchase, but please note that rooting a device or installing unofficial firmware voids our warranty coverage, since we're unable to diagnose or guarantee hardware issues on a device running modified, unsupported software. See the Warranty Policy article for details on what voids coverage.

**Q: How do I know if a specific refurbished phone will work with 5G on my carrier?**
A: This depends on both the phone's supported cellular bands and your specific carrier's 5G network — contact support with the exact model and your carrier before ordering if 5G compatibility is important to confirm ahead of purchase.
