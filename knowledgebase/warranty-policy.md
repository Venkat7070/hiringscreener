# Warranty Policy

Every device purchased from ReNew Mobile — regardless of cosmetic grade, model, or price — is covered by our standard warranty from the date of delivery. This article explains what's covered, what isn't, how long coverage lasts, and how to file a claim.

## Standard Warranty Coverage

**Length of coverage:** 12 months from the date of delivery, as confirmed by the carrier tracking information for your order.

**What's covered:** Our warranty covers functional hardware defects and failures that occur under normal use, including:

- Battery failing to hold a charge or dropping significantly below its listed health percentage
- Screen malfunctions not caused by physical damage (unresponsive touch areas, display flickering, dead pixels appearing after purchase)
- Camera malfunction (front or rear) not caused by physical damage
- Speaker, microphone, or speakerphone failure
- Charging port failure to charge the device when using a functioning cable and power source
- Button or biometric sensor (Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint) failure
- Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or cellular connectivity failure not caused by carrier network issues
- Any other core hardware function that stops working under normal, non-accidental use

**What we'll do:** For any covered issue, we will first attempt a remote diagnostic with our support team to rule out a software issue, which we can often resolve without needing the device shipped back. If the issue is confirmed to be hardware-related, we will offer, at the customer's choice:

- A free repair, with return shipping paid by ReNew Mobile both ways
- A replacement device of the same model, storage size, color, and grade (subject to availability)
- A partial or full refund if a repair or replacement of the same specifications is not available within a reasonable timeframe

## What's Not Covered Under Warranty

The following are not covered under our standard 12-month warranty:

- **Physical damage** occurring after delivery, including cracked screens, water damage, drops, or bending
- **Damage from unauthorized repairs** — if the device has been opened or repaired by anyone other than ReNew Mobile after delivery, warranty coverage is void
- **Software issues caused by the customer**, such as jailbreaking, rooting, or installing unofficial/unsupported operating system modifications
- **Normal battery degradation over time** beyond the initial health guarantee — batteries naturally lose some capacity with use over months and years, which is expected and not a defect (see our Battery Health article for what's guaranteed at time of purchase versus expected degradation over time)
- **Cosmetic wear consistent with the listed grade** — since cosmetic condition is described accurately at time of sale, normal-for-grade cosmetic wear is not a warranty issue
- **Accessories not manufactured by ReNew Mobile**, such as third-party cases, chargers, or cables purchased separately
- **Devices where the IMEI/serial number has been altered or tampered with**
- **Loss or theft of the device** after delivery

## Extended Warranty Options

At checkout, customers can optionally add Extended Protection, which extends coverage from 12 months to 24 months from the date of delivery, and additionally covers one incident of accidental physical damage (a single cracked screen repair or single water damage incident) during the extended coverage period, subject to a service fee lower than the cost of an out-of-warranty repair. Extended Protection must be purchased at the time of the original order and cannot be added retroactively after checkout is complete.

## How to File a Warranty Claim

1. **Contact support** through the website chat widget, WhatsApp, or by emailing support with your order number and a description of the issue.
2. **Remote diagnostic:** Our support team will walk through basic troubleshooting steps to confirm whether the issue is hardware or software-related. This step is usually quick and can often resolve issues like connectivity glitches or app-related slowdowns without any shipping required.
3. **If hardware repair is needed:** We'll email a prepaid return shipping label. Please back up any data on the device before shipping it in, since repairs may require a factory reset.
4. **Turnaround time:** Once we receive the device, most repairs are completed and reshipped within 5–7 business days. Replacement devices, when chosen instead of a repair, typically ship within 2–3 business days of us receiving the original unit back.
5. **Tracking:** You'll receive tracking information by email once your repaired or replacement device ships back to you.

## Warranty Transferability

Our standard warranty is tied to the original order and is valid for whoever currently possesses the device — it is not required to be the original purchaser to file a claim, as long as the claim is made within the coverage window from the original delivery date and the original order number or purchase receipt can be provided or looked up by device IMEI/serial number.

## Warranty on Repaired or Replaced Devices

If a device is repaired under warranty, the repair does not reset or extend the original 12-month (or 24-month, for Extended Protection) coverage window — coverage continues to run from the original delivery date of the initial order, not from the date of the repair. If a device is fully replaced under warranty (rather than repaired), the replacement device is covered for the remainder of the original coverage window from the original delivery date, and additionally receives its own fresh 90-day coverage on the specific component or issue that caused the original replacement, whichever provides longer protection for that specific issue.

## Diagnosing Software vs. Hardware Issues

A meaningful share of issues that seem like hardware problems are actually resolved through software troubleshooting — a restart, a software update, clearing an app's data, or adjusting a setting. Our support team will always walk through basic troubleshooting first before treating an issue as a hardware warranty claim, both because it's often faster for the customer than shipping a device in, and because it helps us correctly identify genuine hardware defects. See our General FAQ and Troubleshooting article for common troubleshooting steps by symptom.

## Warranty Documentation to Keep

While original packaging isn't required, we recommend keeping your order confirmation email (which contains your order number) accessible for the duration of your warranty coverage, since this is the fastest way for support to look up your purchase date and confirm remaining coverage. If you've misplaced this email, support can typically locate your order using the email address used at checkout, the payment method's last four digits, or the device's IMEI/serial number.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need to keep my original packaging for warranty claims?**
A: No, original packaging is not required for warranty service. You will need the device itself and, ideally, your order number.

**Q: What if my warranty claim is denied because the damage is physical, not a hardware defect?**
A: If a claim is determined to be physical damage rather than a covered hardware defect, we'll let you know and offer a paid out-of-warranty repair quote if you'd like to proceed with a repair at your own cost, or you're welcome to have the device returned to you as-is at no charge.

**Q: Does the warranty cover battery replacement if my battery health drops after 6 months of normal use?**
A: Some gradual battery capacity loss over time is normal and expected, similar to how any lithium-ion battery behaves. If your battery health drops significantly below what's reasonable for the amount of time and normal use since delivery, contact support for an evaluation — this is assessed on a case-by-case basis, comparing reported health percentage against typical usage patterns.

**Q: Can I purchase warranty coverage after my original 12 months has expired?**
A: We do not currently offer warranty renewal or extension after the original coverage period (standard or extended) has expired. Out-of-warranty repairs are available at standard repair pricing — contact support for a quote specific to your device and issue.
