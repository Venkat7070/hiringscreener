# Tablets Product Line

In addition to phones, ReNew Mobile carries a selection of refurbished tablets, primarily iPads and Samsung Galaxy Tab devices. This article covers what's typically available and tablet-specific questions.

## Models Typically Available

- **Apple iPad** — including standard iPad, iPad Air, iPad mini, and iPad Pro lines, across various generations depending on available supply
- **Samsung Galaxy Tab** — including Galaxy Tab S and Galaxy Tab A series, spanning flagship and budget-friendly tiers

As with our phone inventory, exact model, storage, color, connectivity type, and grade availability varies based on incoming supply — each product listing reflects real-time stock for that specific configuration.

## Wi-Fi Only vs. Wi-Fi + Cellular

Tablets are available in two connectivity configurations, and this is one of the most important specs to check before purchasing:

- **Wi-Fi Only** — connects to the internet exclusively through Wi-Fi networks; has no SIM card slot and cannot connect to a cellular data network on its own.
- **Wi-Fi + Cellular** — includes a SIM card slot (and on some models, eSIM support) in addition to Wi-Fi, allowing the tablet to connect to a cellular data plan independently of a Wi-Fi network, similar to a phone.

This is a hardware difference baked in at time of manufacture and cannot be added after the fact — a Wi-Fi Only tablet can never be upgraded to support cellular connectivity. Customers who want to use their tablet away from Wi-Fi networks (for travel, commuting, or as a primary internet-connected device) should specifically choose a Wi-Fi + Cellular listing and confirm cellular band compatibility with their intended carrier before ordering.

## Storage Options

Tablet storage works the same way as phone storage — a fixed capacity set at time of manufacture, shown on each listing, and not upgradable after purchase except on models that support microSD expansion (noted on the specific listing when applicable — most iPads do not support microSD expansion, while some Samsung Galaxy Tab A series models do).

## Stylus and Keyboard Accessory Support

Many tablets support an optional stylus and/or keyboard accessory:

- **Apple Pencil** — supported by iPad models depending on generation; note that different iPad generations support different Apple Pencil generations (1st gen vs. 2nd gen vs. USB-C Pencil), which are not interchangeable across all models. Check the specific listing for which Apple Pencil generation (if any) is compatible with that model.
- **S Pen** — included with or supported by Samsung Galaxy Tab S series models (varies by specific model whether the S Pen is included in the box or sold separately)
- **Keyboard accessories** — such as Apple's Magic Keyboard or Smart Keyboard Folio for iPad, or Samsung's Book Cover Keyboard for Galaxy Tab — are almost always sold separately and not included with the tablet itself unless explicitly listed as included on the product page

Styluses and keyboard accessories, when not explicitly included with a listing, can be purchased separately through our accessories catalog if available, or through the original manufacturer.

## Battery Health on Tablets

The same 85% minimum battery health guarantee that applies to our phones also applies to every tablet we sell, tested during our refurbishment process the same way. See our Battery Health and Device Care article for more detail on how this is measured and what to expect from tablet batteries over time — tablet batteries generally degrade more slowly than phone batteries due to typically lighter, less continuous daily use patterns, though this varies by how the individual tablet was used by its previous owner.

## Account Locks on Tablets

The same account lock protections described for phones apply equally to tablets — iPads are checked and cleared of iCloud Activation Lock, and Android tablets are checked and cleared of Google Factory Reset Protection (and Samsung Reactivation Lock, for Samsung Galaxy Tab devices), before any unit is listed for sale. As with phones, if a customer ever encounters an account lock screen during tablet setup, this should be reported to support immediately for an immediate replacement or refund at no cost.

## Operating System and Updates

iPads run iPadOS, and receive software updates the same way iPhones receive iOS updates — through Settings, with update availability and support length determined by Apple based on the specific model. Samsung Galaxy Tab devices run Android (with Samsung's One UI interface layered on top), with update support length determined by Samsung, generally following similar patterns to Samsung's phone update policies.

## Included Accessories

Refurbished tablets ship with a charging cable matching that model's port, but do not include a wall power adapter unless explicitly listed as included — consistent with our phone accessory policy. Styluses and keyboard accessories are only included when explicitly stated on the specific product listing.

## Common Compatibility Questions

**Can I make phone calls on a Wi-Fi + Cellular tablet?**
Generally no — Wi-Fi + Cellular tablets use their cellular connection for data (internet access) only, not traditional cellular voice calling, though some models support internet-based calling apps over their data connection. This is different from a phone's cellular capability. Check the specific model's specifications if voice calling capability is a requirement.

**Will my old tablet case or keyboard accessory fit a refurbished tablet of the same model?**
Yes, as long as it's the exact same model and generation — accessories are generally model-specific and don't fit across different generations of the same product line (for example, a case for one iPad generation typically won't fit a different iPad generation, even within the same product name), due to dimension and camera cutout changes between generations.

**Can I use a SIM card from my phone in a Wi-Fi + Cellular tablet?**
This depends on your carrier's plan — some carriers require a separate data-only SIM or plan add-on for a tablet, rather than allowing a phone's SIM to simply be moved over. Check with your specific carrier about tablet data plan options before assuming your existing phone SIM will work directly in a tablet.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do refurbished tablets come with a manufacturer warranty?**
A: No — manufacturer warranties are tied to the original purchaser and point of sale and do not transfer to secondhand devices. All ReNew Mobile tablets are instead covered under our own 12-month warranty described in the Warranty Policy article.

**Q: Is a Wi-Fi + Cellular tablet more expensive than the same tablet in Wi-Fi Only?**
A: Generally yes, since Wi-Fi + Cellular models include additional hardware (a modem and SIM slot) not present in Wi-Fi Only models — pricing for each configuration is shown separately on our listings when both are available for a given model.

**Q: Can I add a cellular data plan to my tablet with any carrier?**
A: This depends on both the tablet's supported cellular bands and which carriers support tablet data plans in your area — contact your preferred carrier directly to ask about tablet data plan options and confirm compatibility with the specific tablet model before ordering if this is important to you.
