# About ReNew Mobile and Our Refurbishment Process

## Who We Are

ReNew Mobile is an online retailer specializing in high-quality refurbished and renewed mobile phones and tablets. We source pre-owned devices, restore them to like-new condition through a rigorous multi-point inspection and repair process, and resell them at a fraction of the cost of buying new — without sacrificing reliability or performance.

Our mission is simple: make great technology more affordable and more sustainable. Every device we sell has already been manufactured, which means choosing a refurbished phone or tablet from ReNew Mobile keeps a working device out of a landfill and reduces the demand for new raw materials, rare earth metals, and manufacturing emissions associated with producing a brand-new unit.

We are not a marketplace connecting random third-party sellers to buyers. Every device listed on our site passes through our own refurbishment facility, where trained technicians inspect, repair, clean, and test it before it's approved for sale. This is what separates a "used" phone bought from an individual seller — with no guarantees — from a "refurbished" phone bought from ReNew Mobile, which comes backed by warranty, quality testing, and customer support.

## Where Our Devices Come From

We source inventory from several channels:

- **Carrier and retailer trade-in programs** — devices traded in by customers upgrading to newer models.
- **Corporate and enterprise buybacks** — businesses refreshing employee device fleets.
- **Certified wholesale partners** — vetted suppliers who provide bulk pre-owned inventory that we inspect independently before accepting.
- **Manufacturer-certified returns** — devices returned within a return window that were never fully activated or used, sometimes called "open box" units.

We do not source devices from unknown individual sellers, pawn shops, or unverified marketplaces. Every unit that enters our facility is checked against stolen-device databases (IMEI/serial blacklist checks) before it enters our refurbishment pipeline. Devices that fail this check are rejected and never listed for sale.

## The ReNew Mobile Refurbishment Process

Every device goes through the same standardized process, regardless of brand or model. This typically takes our technicians between 45 minutes and 3 hours per device, depending on condition and repairs required.

### Step 1: Intake and Blacklist Check

When a device arrives at our facility, it's logged into our inventory system by IMEI (phones) or serial number (tablets). We immediately run this identifier against national and international blacklist databases to confirm the device has not been reported lost, stolen, or is not still financed/locked to an account. Devices that fail this check are quarantined and never enter our sellable inventory.

### Step 2: Full Diagnostic Testing

Each device runs through a 40+ point diagnostic covering:

- **Display** — dead pixels, touch responsiveness, brightness uniformity, screen burn-in (for OLED panels)
- **Battery health** — maximum capacity as a percentage of original design capacity, charging cycle count, and charging behavior (wired and wireless where applicable)
- **Cameras** — front and rear camera functionality, autofocus, flash, and image quality across lenses
- **Speakers and microphones** — call audio, speakerphone, and microphone clarity on both ends of a test call
- **Buttons and haptics** — power, volume, home button (where applicable), and haptic vibration motor
- **Biometrics** — Face ID, fingerprint sensors, and Touch ID functionality
- **Connectivity** — Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular signal reception, GPS accuracy, and NFC (for tap-to-pay and file transfer)
- **Ports** — charging port function and physical condition, headphone jack where present
- **Software** — confirming the device is not carrier-locked (unless explicitly listed as carrier-locked), is not tied to a previous owner's account (iCloud Activation Lock, Google Factory Reset Protection, or Samsung Reactivation Lock), and can be freshly set up

### Step 3: Data Wipe and Factory Reset

Before any repair work begins, every device undergoes a certified data wipe following NIST 800-88 data sanitization guidelines. This permanently erases all data from the previous owner — photos, messages, accounts, app data, and browsing history. We also manually confirm that all account locks (iCloud, Google, Samsung) have been removed, since a device with an active activation lock cannot be resold or used by a new owner.

### Step 4: Cosmetic Inspection and Grading

Once functionality is confirmed, the device is cosmetically inspected under bright, even lighting from multiple angles. Screen, back glass/housing, camera lens, and edges/frame are each independently assessed. This inspection determines the device's cosmetic grade — see our separate Grading & Condition Guide article for the full breakdown of what Grade A ("Excellent"), Grade B ("Good"), and Grade C ("Fair") mean.

### Step 5: Repairs

Any component that fails diagnostic testing is either repaired or the part is replaced, depending on the issue:

- Cracked or scratched screens are replaced with quality-tested replacement displays
- Batteries that test below 85% of original design capacity are replaced with new batteries
- Damaged charging ports, buttons, or cameras are repaired using OEM or OEM-equivalent parts
- Housings and back glass with significant damage are replaced where the device design allows

We do not sell devices with cracked screens, non-functional cameras, unreliable charging ports, or batteries below our minimum health threshold. If a device cannot be repaired to meet our functional standards, it is either used for parts in our repair inventory or responsibly recycled through a certified e-waste partner — never resold as a working unit.

### Step 6: Deep Cleaning and Sanitization

Every device is disassembled where necessary and thoroughly cleaned — screen, ports, speaker grilles, camera lenses, buttons, and housing. This isn't a wipe-down; it's a genuine deep clean to remove dust, debris, and residue from prior use. Devices are also sanitized before packaging.

### Step 7: Final Quality Assurance Pass

After repairs and cleaning, the device goes through a second, independent QA pass by a different technician than the one who performed the repairs. This second set of eyes re-runs the full diagnostic suite to confirm everything still functions correctly after reassembly, and re-confirms the cosmetic grade assigned in Step 4 is still accurate.

### Step 8: Fresh Setup and Packaging

Devices are reset to factory settings one final time, software is updated to the latest compatible operating system version where possible, and the device is powered off for shipping. Each unit ships with a charging cable, and select listings include additional accessories as noted on the product page. Devices are packaged in protective materials designed to prevent shipping damage.

## What "Refurbished" Means at ReNew Mobile

We use the terms "refurbished" and "renewed" interchangeably to describe the same standard: a pre-owned device that has been professionally inspected, repaired as needed, data-wiped, cleaned, and quality-tested before resale. This is different from:

- **"Used" or "as-is"** — sold with no guarantee of functionality, no data wipe confirmation, and no warranty. We do not sell devices this way.
- **"Refurbished by manufacturer"** — devices refurbished directly by Apple, Samsung, or Google through their own official programs. We are an independent refurbisher and are not affiliated with these manufacturers; our devices are not manufacturer-certified refurbished units unless explicitly stated on the product listing.

Every device sold by ReNew Mobile includes our own quality guarantee and warranty, described in our Warranty Policy article, regardless of grading tier.

## Environmental Impact

Manufacturing a single new smartphone generates significant carbon emissions, largely from raw material extraction and component manufacturing — far more than the emissions generated from using the device over its lifetime. By purchasing a refurbished device instead of new, customers meaningfully reduce e-waste and the demand for newly mined materials. ReNew Mobile publishes an estimated environmental savings figure on select product pages based on independent lifecycle assessment studies for consumer electronics.

Devices that fail our refurbishment standards and cannot be safely resold are broken down for usable parts (screens, batteries, cameras, and other components are reused in future repairs) or sent to certified e-waste recycling partners who ensure hazardous materials are handled safely and valuable materials are recovered.

## Questions About a Specific Device

If a customer asks about the condition or history of a specific device beyond what's listed on the product page, support agents should reference the device's listed grade and the standard refurbishment process described above. We do not have device-specific repair logs available to share with customers, since our process is standardized across all units of the same grade.
