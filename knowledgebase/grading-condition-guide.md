# Device Grading and Condition Guide

Every device sold by ReNew Mobile is assigned a cosmetic condition grade after passing our full functional refurbishment process. This guide explains exactly what each grade means, so customers know what to expect before their device arrives.

**Important: grading only describes cosmetic appearance, not functionality.** Every device, regardless of grade, has passed the same 40+ point functional diagnostic and is guaranteed to work correctly. A Grade C device has more visible wear than a Grade A device, but both have a fully functional screen, battery, cameras, and buttons. We never sell a device with a cracked screen, a battery below our minimum health threshold, or non-functional hardware, at any grade.

## Grade A — Excellent Condition

**What it looks like:** Grade A devices show minimal to no visible signs of use. There may be very light, faint micro-scratches visible only under close inspection in direct light, but there is no denting, no visible scuffing on the frame, and no cracks or chips anywhere on the device. The screen has no visible scratches when the display is on and in normal use.

**Who it's for:** Customers who want their refurbished device to look and feel as close to new as possible, and who are comfortable paying a modest premium over Grade B for that appearance.

**What's included:** Grade A devices go through an additional cosmetic polish pass during refurbishment to minimize any remaining surface marks.

## Grade B — Good Condition

**What it looks like:** Grade B devices show normal signs of prior use. This typically includes light scratches on the back or frame that are visible in direct light, and possibly very minor, shallow scuffs on the edges from normal handling. The screen may have extremely light micro-scratches that are not typically visible or felt during normal use, but do not affect touch response or display clarity.

**Who it's for:** The majority of our customers choose Grade B — it offers the best balance of price and appearance, since the cosmetic wear is minor and often not noticeable once the device is in a case (which we recommend for any phone or tablet).

**What's included:** Grade B is our standard grading tier and represents the largest share of our inventory.

## Grade C — Fair Condition

**What it looks like:** Grade C devices show more noticeable signs of prior use. This can include visible scratches on the back and frame, small dents or dings on corners that don't affect structural integrity, and visible scratches on the screen that are noticeable in certain lighting but do not affect touch responsiveness, display brightness, or color accuracy. Grade C devices are fully functional and pass the same diagnostic testing as every other grade — the wear is purely cosmetic.

**Who it's for:** Budget-conscious customers who prioritize the lowest price and don't mind visible cosmetic wear, especially those who plan to use a case and screen protector regardless of a device's starting condition.

**What's included:** Grade C represents the best value in our catalog for customers focused purely on function over form.

## What Every Grade Guarantees, Regardless of Tier

Regardless of which grade a customer purchases, every ReNew Mobile device is guaranteed to have:

- A fully functional display with accurate touch response and no dead pixels
- A battery that tests at or above 85% of its original design capacity (see our Battery Health article for details)
- Fully functional front and rear cameras, including autofocus and flash
- Working speakers, microphone, and speakerphone
- Functional biometric authentication (Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint sensor, as applicable to the model)
- A working charging port and successful charging test, wired and wireless where the model supports it
- Confirmed removal of all account locks (iCloud Activation Lock, Google FRP, Samsung Reactivation Lock)
- A confirmed IMEI/serial blacklist check showing no lost, stolen, or financed status
- A certified data wipe confirming no data from the previous owner remains on the device
- Our standard warranty coverage (see the Warranty Policy article)

## How Grading Differs From Battery Health

Grading and battery health are graded and reported completely independently. A Grade A device does not automatically have a higher battery health percentage than a Grade C device, and vice versa. Battery health is listed separately on every product page as a specific percentage (for example, "Battery Health: 90%+"), since it's a functional spec rather than a cosmetic one. Customers who specifically want the best possible battery life should check the listed battery health percentage on the product page rather than assuming it correlates with cosmetic grade.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I request a specific grade if I'm not sure which one is in stock for my desired model?**
A: Product pages only list grades that are currently in stock for that specific model, storage size, and color combination. If a grade isn't shown as available, it's out of stock for that configuration and may become available again as new inventory is processed.

**Q: Does a lower grade mean the device is older or has fewer features?**
A: No. Grading is entirely about cosmetic wear, not the age, model, or feature set of the device. A Grade C device of a given model has the exact same features, chip, camera hardware, and specifications as a Grade A device of that same model — only the cosmetic appearance differs.

**Q: Will a screen protector or case hide grading imperfections?**
A: In most cases, yes. A case will fully cover frame and back scratches for Grade B and Grade C devices, and a screen protector will minimize the visibility and feel of any light screen scratches on Grade B and Grade C units. We recommend both regardless of which grade you choose, both to protect your device going forward and to minimize the visibility of any existing cosmetic wear.

**Q: What if my device arrives and doesn't match its listed grade?**
A: Contact our support team within 30 days of delivery with photos of the device. If we confirm the unit doesn't match its listed grade, we'll offer a partial refund reflecting the correct grade, a replacement unit at the correct grade if available, or a full refund — customer's choice.
