# Order Tracking and Account Help

This article covers how to manage your ReNew Mobile account, track orders, and resolve common account-related issues.

## Creating an Account

Customers can check out either as a guest or by creating an account. Creating an account allows you to:

- View your full order history in one place
- Track current and past shipments without needing to search your email for a tracking link
- Save shipping addresses and (where supported by your payment method) save payment information securely for faster future checkout
- Access warranty and return information tied to your past purchases more easily, since our support team can look up your order history directly from your account

Guest checkout is fully supported for customers who prefer not to create an account — order confirmation, tracking, and receipt information is still sent by email, and warranty or return requests can still be made using your order number, even without an account.

## Converting a Guest Order to an Account

If you checked out as a guest and later want to create an account using the same email address, your past guest orders placed under that email will automatically appear in your new account's order history once the account is created and verified.

## Tracking an Order

There are three ways to track an order:

1. **Shipping confirmation email** — sent once your order ships, containing a tracking number and direct tracking link.
2. **Account order history** — if you have an account, log in and view Order History for real-time status and tracking links for all your orders in one place.
3. **Contact support** — provide your order number (found in your order confirmation email) and we can look up current status and tracking information directly.

## Order Status Meanings

- **Processing** — your order has been received and is being prepared for shipment (quality re-check, packaging).
- **Shipped** — your order has been handed to the shipping carrier and tracking information is now active.
- **Out for Delivery** — the carrier has the package on a delivery vehicle for delivery that day.
- **Delivered** — the carrier has marked the package as delivered to the shipping address.
- **Delayed** — an issue has been identified with the shipment (carrier delay, weather, or similar); support will typically reach out proactively if this status appears on your order for an extended period.

## Updating Account Information

Logged-in customers can update their name, email address, saved shipping addresses, and (depending on payment method) saved payment information directly through their Account Settings page. If you're unable to access your account to make a change, or no longer have access to the email address on file, contact support to verify your identity and assist with the update.

## Password Reset

If you've forgotten your account password, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page, which sends a password reset link to the email address associated with your account. If you no longer have access to that email address, contact support for identity verification and manual account recovery assistance.

## Order History and Receipts

Your full order history, including itemized receipts for tax or expense purposes, is available in your account under Order History. Guest checkout customers can request a copy of a specific order's receipt by contacting support with the order number or the email address used at checkout.

## Managing Saved Payment Methods

Depending on the payment method originally used, some payment details may be securely saved to your account (through our PCI-compliant payment processor) for faster future checkout. Saved payment methods can be viewed, added, or removed under Account Settings > Payment Methods. We do not have the ability to view full saved card numbers ourselves — only the payment processor holds that information securely.

## Deleting Your Account

Customers who wish to permanently delete their ReNew Mobile account and associated personal data can request this by contacting support. Deleting your account does not affect the warranty coverage on devices you've already purchased — warranty claims can still be processed using your order number even without an active account, since warranty coverage is tied to the device and order, not to account status.

## Common Login Issues

**"Email already in use" when creating an account:** This typically means an account already exists for that email address, often from a previous guest checkout that was later formalized, or a past account creation that was forgotten. Try the "Forgot Password" flow instead of creating a new account.

**Not receiving password reset or verification emails:** Check your spam or promotions folder first. If the email still isn't found, confirm you're checking the correct email address, and contact support if the issue continues, since we can manually verify the email on file for your account.

**Order not appearing in account history:** This can happen if an order was placed as a guest under a different email address than the one associated with your account, or if it was placed while logged out. Contact support with the order number and we can help link it to your account if needed.

## Email Notifications

Account holders and guest checkout customers alike receive email notifications at key points in the order lifecycle: order confirmation immediately after purchase, shipping confirmation with tracking once the order ships, and a delivery confirmation once the carrier marks the package delivered. Logged-in customers can manage which of these notifications they receive (for example, opting out of marketing emails while keeping transactional order emails) under Account Settings > Notification Preferences. Transactional emails directly related to an active order — confirmation, shipping, and delivery updates — cannot be disabled, since they contain information needed to track and receive your order.

## Multiple Shipping Addresses

Accounts can save multiple shipping addresses (for example, a home address and a work address) and select which one to use at checkout for each order. There's no limit on the number of saved addresses. Saved addresses can be edited or removed at any time under Account Settings > Addresses, and removing a saved address does not affect any past order that already shipped to it.

## Linking a Trade-In to Your Account

If you've submitted a device through our Trade-In Program, the trade-in's status (quote submitted, device received, inspection complete, credit issued) is visible in your account if you were logged in when you started the trade-in, or if you provide the same email address used during the trade-in process. See the Trade-In Program article for the full trade-in process and timeline.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I combine multiple separate orders into one shipment?**
A: Orders are generally processed and shipped independently based on when they were placed and current inventory processing, so separate orders typically ship separately even if placed close together. Contact support promptly after placing a second order if you'd like us to check whether combining shipment is possible before either order ships — this isn't always possible depending on timing.

**Q: How long is my order history kept?**
A: Order history is retained in your account indefinitely for as long as your account remains active, so you can always reference past purchases, warranty status, and receipts.

**Q: Can someone else pick up or manage my order on my behalf?**
A: For security reasons, account and order details are only discussed directly with the account holder or the individual named on the original order, unless you've explicitly authorized someone else in writing (for example, via a message to support naming who else may act on your behalf for that specific order).
