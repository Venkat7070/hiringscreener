# Returns and Refunds Policy

We want customers to be genuinely happy with their refurbished device. This article covers our return window, the return process, refund timelines, and situations that fall outside our standard return policy.

## Return Window

Customers have **30 days from the date of delivery** to return a device for any reason, for a full refund, provided the device is returned in the same condition it was received (see "Condition of Returned Devices" below).

This 30-day window applies to standard change-of-mind returns as well as returns due to a device not matching its listed grade, specification, or condition.

## How to Start a Return

1. **Contact support** via the website chat widget, WhatsApp, or email, and provide your order number along with the reason for the return. This helps us process the return correctly and improve our listings and process going forward.
2. **Receive a prepaid return label.** Once a return is approved, we email a prepaid shipping label — customers are never asked to pay for standard return shipping within the 30-day window.
3. **Back up and factory reset the device.** Before shipping the device back, please sign out of any accounts (Apple ID / Google account) and perform a factory reset if possible. This isn't strictly required for us to process the return, but it protects your personal data and speeds up processing on our end. If a device is returned still signed into a personal account with Activation Lock or a similar lock enabled, this can delay refund processing since we're unable to test the returned unit until the lock is removed.
4. **Package the device securely,** ideally in the original packaging, and drop it off using the prepaid label provided.
5. **Refund processing.** Once we receive and inspect the returned device, we'll process your refund — see timelines below.

## Condition of Returned Devices

To qualify for a full refund, a returned device should be in the same condition as when it was delivered — no new physical damage, and all included accessories (charging cable, any included accessories listed on the original order) returned along with the device.

- **Devices returned with new physical damage** (cracks, water damage, etc. not present at delivery) may be subject to a partial refund reflecting a repair cost deduction, rather than a full refund.
- **Devices returned without included accessories** may have the value of the missing accessory deducted from the refund.
- **Devices still locked to a previous account** (Activation Lock, Google FRP, Samsung Reactivation Lock) cannot be fully processed until the lock is removed — our support team will reach out with instructions if this happens.

## Refund Timelines

- **Processing time:** Once we receive a returned device, inspection and refund approval typically takes 2–3 business days.
- **Refund method:** Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout.
- **Bank/card processing time:** After we issue the refund, most banks and card issuers post the credit within 3–7 business days, though this varies by financial institution and is outside our control.
- **Order confirmation email:** You'll receive an email confirming the refund has been issued, which includes the amount refunded and the date it was processed on our end.

## What's Refunded

A standard return within the 30-day window refunds the full purchase price of the device, including any add-on protection plans purchased with it (since those plans only apply to a device you keep). Original outbound shipping costs are non-refundable in change-of-mind returns, unless the return is due to our error (wrong item shipped, device not matching its listed condition or specifications, or a defective unit). In those cases, all shipping costs — both original and return — are covered by ReNew Mobile and the full order amount is refunded.

## Exchanges

If a customer wants a different model, storage size, color, or grade rather than a refund, this is handled as a return for refund followed by a new order for the desired item, since our returns process is built around individual device inventory rather than pooled stock swaps. Support can help set up the new order at the same time as processing the return so there's no gap in getting a working device to you — for example, we can ship the replacement device before receiving the returned one back, at our discretion, for customers in good standing.

## Situations Outside Standard Returns

- **Returns after 30 days from delivery:** Once the 30-day window has passed, a device is no longer eligible for a standard return, but is still covered under our 12-month warranty for hardware defects — see the Warranty Policy article for that process instead.
- **Devices purchased through a third-party marketplace** (if ReNew Mobile inventory is ever listed on an external platform): returns for those purchases should go through that platform's return process rather than directly with us, since the marketplace handles payment and logistics for those orders.
- **Gift purchases:** If a device was purchased as a gift, the recipient can initiate a return within 30 days of the delivery date (not the date they received it as a gift, if different) by contacting support with the order number, which the original purchaser can share with them.

## Cancelling an Order Before Delivery

If you'd like to cancel an order before it ships, contact support as soon as possible — orders that haven't yet shipped can typically be cancelled with a full and immediate refund, no return shipping required. Once an order has shipped, it's no longer possible to cancel it in transit, but you're welcome to refuse delivery or initiate a standard return once it arrives.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I get a full refund if I just changed my mind and the device works perfectly?**
A: Yes. Our 30-day return window covers change-of-mind returns for any reason, not just defects, as long as the device is returned in the condition it was received in.

**Q: What if I'm past 30 days but the device just started having a problem?**
A: That's exactly what our 12-month warranty covers — contact support and we'll open a warranty claim instead of a return. See the Warranty Policy article.

**Q: Will I be charged a restocking fee?**
A: No, we do not charge restocking fees on standard returns within the 30-day window when the device is returned in the condition it was delivered in.

**Q: I received the wrong device — what do I do?**
A: Contact support immediately with your order number and a photo of what you received. We'll arrange a prepaid return of the incorrect item and expedite shipping of the correct one at no additional cost.
