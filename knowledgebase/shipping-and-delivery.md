# Shipping and Delivery

This article covers shipping methods, costs, delivery timelines, order tracking, and what to do if there's a shipping issue.

## Domestic Shipping Options

We offer the following shipping speeds on domestic orders. Exact carrier used may vary by destination and package weight, but delivery windows are consistent across carriers we work with.

- **Standard Shipping (Free):** Estimated delivery in 4–6 business days from when the order ships. Free on all orders with no minimum purchase required.
- **Expedited Shipping:** Estimated delivery in 2–3 business days from when the order ships. Flat rate fee shown at checkout, which varies slightly by destination.
- **Express Shipping:** Estimated delivery in 1–2 business days from when the order ships, for customers who need their device quickly. Flat rate fee shown at checkout, higher than Expedited.

All shipping estimates are business days and do not include weekends or major holidays. Delivery estimates begin from the day an order ships, not the day it's placed — see "Processing Time" below for how long it typically takes an order to ship after purchase.

## Processing Time

Most orders are processed and handed to the shipping carrier within **1 business day** of purchase, since our refurbished inventory is already in stock and quality-tested at the time it's listed for sale. Orders placed after our daily processing cutoff (shown at checkout) are processed the following business day. We do not ship on weekends or major holidays, though online orders can be placed any time and will be processed on the next business day.

During high-demand periods (such as major holiday shopping seasons), processing time may extend to 1–2 business days — any such delay will be noted with a banner on the site if it's currently in effect.

## International Shipping

We currently ship to a limited set of international destinations. International shipping costs and delivery estimates are calculated at checkout based on the destination and are typically longer than domestic timelines — generally 7–14 business days depending on destination and customs processing. International orders may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, or fees charged by the destination country, which are the responsibility of the customer and are not included in the price shown at checkout, unless explicitly noted as "duties included" on the product page.

Devices sold to international destinations should be checked for compatibility with local cellular network bands before ordering — see the product page for each listing's supported network compatibility, or contact support before ordering if you're unsure whether a device will work with your local carrier.

## Order Tracking

Once an order ships, customers receive a shipping confirmation email containing a tracking number and a link to track the package directly with the carrier. Tracking information is also available at any time by logging into your ReNew Mobile account and viewing your order history, or by contacting support with your order number.

Tracking updates can occasionally take 12–24 hours to first appear after a shipping label is created, since this reflects the time between us handing the package to the carrier and the carrier's first scan.

## Signature Requirements

Orders above a certain value threshold (shown at checkout if it applies to your order) require an adult signature upon delivery, as a security measure given the resale value of the devices we ship. If no one is available to sign, the carrier will typically leave a delivery attempt notice with instructions for redelivery or pickup at a local facility.

## Delivery Address Changes

If you need to change your shipping address after placing an order, contact support as soon as possible. We can update the address if the order hasn't yet shipped. Once an order has shipped, we're unable to redirect it ourselves, but in many cases the shipping carrier offers an address change or hold-for-pickup option directly through their own tracking portal — check the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email for these options.

## What to Do If Your Package Is Delayed

If tracking shows no movement for more than 3 business days, or the estimated delivery date has passed without the package arriving, contact support with your order number. We'll investigate with the carrier and either provide an updated status or, if the package appears lost in transit, begin a replacement or refund process — customers are never responsible for packages lost in transit while in the carrier's possession.

## What to Do If Your Package Arrives Damaged

If a package arrives with visible external damage, we recommend taking photos of the packaging before opening it, then inspecting the device itself. If the device is damaged, contact support within 48 hours of delivery with photos of both the packaging and the device — damaged-in-transit claims are handled at no cost to the customer, with a replacement or refund issued once the claim is reviewed, and no return shipping charge for the damaged unit.

## Signature and Delivery Confirmation Disputes

In the rare case that tracking shows a package as "delivered" but the customer has not received it, contact support promptly. We'll work with the carrier to investigate (including checking for delivery to a neighboring address or a held-for-pickup status) and follow up with next steps, which may include a replacement or refund depending on the outcome of the carrier's investigation.

## Packaging and Security

Devices are shipped in plain, non-descript outer packaging that does not indicate the contents or value of the package, as a security precaution given the resale value of the devices we ship. Inside, each device is individually packaged in protective materials designed to prevent movement and impact damage during transit. This plain outer packaging is intentional and not a sign of a lower-quality or lower-priority order.

## Multiple Items and Split Shipments

If an order contains multiple devices or accessories, items are generally shipped together in a single package when inventory allows. If one item in a multi-item order is temporarily out of stock or requires additional processing time, the order may be split into multiple shipments so that available items aren't held up waiting on a single delayed item — in this case, each shipment gets its own tracking number, and you'll be notified by email when a split shipment occurs.

## Holiday and Peak Season Shipping

During major holiday periods, both our processing times and carrier delivery times can be longer than the standard estimates listed above, due to increased order volume across the shipping industry generally, not something specific to ReNew Mobile. We recommend ordering earlier than usual around major shopping holidays if a delivery date matters for gifting purposes, and we'll post any known extended processing delays as a banner on the site during these periods.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I pick up my order in person instead of having it shipped?**
A: We are an online-only retailer and do not currently offer in-person pickup at a physical location.

**Q: Can I request a specific delivery date?**
A: We're unable to guarantee specific delivery dates, but Express Shipping offers the fastest and most predictable delivery window if timing is important.

**Q: Do you ship to PO boxes?**
A: This depends on the carrier and shipping method selected — some expedited and express shipping options require a physical street address. If you're shipping to a PO box, Standard Shipping is the most likely option to be available; check at checkout for what's offered for your specific address.

**Q: My tracking says "out for delivery" but it's late in the day — should I be worried?**
A: Not necessarily — carriers sometimes deliver later in the day than expected, especially during high-volume periods. If the package hasn't arrived by the end of the day it was marked "out for delivery," check again the following morning before contacting support, since carriers occasionally correct next-day.
