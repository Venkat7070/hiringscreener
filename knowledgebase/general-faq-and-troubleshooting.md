# General FAQ and Basic Troubleshooting

This article collects frequently asked questions that don't fit neatly into our other knowledge base topics, along with basic troubleshooting steps support can walk customers through before escalating to a hardware warranty claim.

## Basic Troubleshooting Before Assuming a Hardware Issue

Many issues customers report as "broken" or "defective" can be resolved with basic troubleshooting, which support should walk through before treating something as a warranty claim:

**Device won't turn on:**
1. Confirm the device is actually charging — try a different cable and power outlet, and let it charge for at least 30 minutes before trying to power on again, since a fully drained battery can sometimes take a few minutes of charging before the device is responsive enough to show any charging indicator.
2. Try a forced restart using the model-specific button combination (this varies by device — support can look up the exact combination for the customer's specific model).
3. If the device still won't power on after confirming charging is working, this is treated as a hardware issue and a warranty claim should be opened.

**Wi-Fi or Bluetooth not connecting:**
1. Confirm Wi-Fi/Bluetooth is toggled on in device settings.
2. Restart the device.
3. Forget the network and reconnect, entering the password fresh.
4. Test connecting to a different Wi-Fi network or Bluetooth device, to rule out an issue with the specific network/accessory rather than the phone or tablet itself.
5. If connectivity fails across multiple different networks/devices after a restart, this points to a hardware issue and a warranty claim should be opened.

**App crashes or device running slowly:**
1. Restart the device — this resolves the majority of temporary software slowdowns.
2. Check for pending software updates and install them.
3. Check available storage space — a device that's nearly full on storage can run slowly; freeing up space often resolves this.
4. If a specific single app is crashing, try deleting and reinstalling that app, since this is usually an app-specific issue rather than a device issue.
5. General device-wide slowness after ruling out the above is not typically a hardware warranty issue, since our refurbishment process includes wiping and (where beneficial) updating the operating system — ongoing performance depends significantly on how many apps and how much data accumulates on the device over time during ownership, same as any device.

**Battery draining unusually fast:**
1. Check battery usage by app (Settings > Battery on most devices) to identify whether a specific app is using disproportionate battery in the background.
2. Confirm background app refresh / background data settings aren't set to allow every app to run freely in the background.
3. Check battery health percentage (see the Battery Health and Device Care article) — if battery health itself tests well but daily battery life still feels short, this is more likely a usage/settings issue than a hardware defect.
4. If battery health tests below what's expected and drains rapidly even with minimal use, this points to a hardware issue and a warranty claim should be considered.

**Screen unresponsive in certain areas:**
1. Remove any screen protector and retest, since a poorly applied or damaged screen protector can sometimes interfere with touch responsiveness.
2. Restart the device.
3. If specific areas of the screen remain consistently unresponsive after removing any screen protector and restarting, this is a hardware issue and a warranty claim should be opened.

## Setting Up a New Device

**Do I need to do anything before setting up my refurbished device?**
No special steps are required — every device we sell has already been factory reset, data-wiped, and confirmed free of any account locks, so it's ready for normal first-time setup exactly as if it were new out of the box. Simply power it on and follow the on-screen setup prompts.

**Can I transfer data from my old device?**
Yes — both iOS and Android support standard data transfer methods during initial setup (device-to-device transfer, cloud backup restore, or computer-based backup restore, depending on platform). See the iPhone Product Line or Android Phones Product Line articles for platform-specific transfer details.

**The device is asking me to update software during setup — is that normal?**
Yes, this is normal and expected. While we install the latest compatible software during our refurbishment process, additional updates may have been released by the manufacturer between when the device was refurbished and when it reaches you. Installing this update is safe and recommended.

## Device Condition and Authenticity Questions

**How do I know the device I receive is genuine and not counterfeit?**
Every device we sell is sourced through verified channels (trade-in programs, corporate buybacks, and vetted wholesale partners) and inspected as part of our refurbishment process, which would identify a counterfeit or non-genuine device, since it would fail our diagnostic and software verification checks. See the About Us and Refurbishment Process article for full sourcing detail.

**Will the device have any of the previous owner's data on it?**
No — every device undergoes a certified data wipe following NIST 800-88 data sanitization guidelines as a mandatory step in our refurbishment process, before any repair work even begins. This is independently confirmed during our final quality assurance pass as well.

**Can I find out who owned the device before me?**
No, we do not retain or share information about a device's previous owner, both for privacy reasons and because our sourcing process (trade-ins, buybacks, wholesale partners) doesn't provide us with that level of individual ownership history in most cases.

## Contacting Support

**What are the best ways to reach support?**
Support is available through the website chat widget, WhatsApp, and email. Response times are generally fastest through website chat and WhatsApp for straightforward questions, while email is well-suited for issues requiring photos or detailed documentation (such as a return or warranty claim).

**What information should I have ready when contacting support?**
Having your order number ready speeds up most support interactions significantly, since it lets us pull up your specific order, device, and purchase date immediately. For a warranty or return issue, having photos of the device or issue ready to share is also helpful.

**Do you offer phone support?**
Support is currently handled through chat-based channels (website widget, WhatsApp, and email) rather than phone calls, which allows us to keep detailed written records of every conversation for follow-up and warranty/return documentation purposes.

## Escalation Guidance for Support Agents

If a customer's question or issue doesn't clearly fall under troubleshooting, warranty, returns, shipping, or another specific knowledge base topic, or if a customer expresses significant frustration, requests a manager, or describes a situation involving potential fraud or a safety concern, this should be escalated to a human support agent rather than resolved automatically — acknowledge the customer's concern, let them know a team member will follow up, and avoid making commitments (refund amounts, replacement timelines, or exceptions to stated policy) beyond what's documented in this knowledge base without human review.
