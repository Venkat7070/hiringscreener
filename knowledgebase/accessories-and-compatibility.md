# Accessories and Compatibility

In addition to refurbished phones and tablets, ReNew Mobile sells a range of accessories, and we frequently field questions about what's included with a device versus what needs to be purchased separately. This article clarifies our accessory policies and common compatibility questions across our catalog.

## What's Included With Every Device

Every refurbished phone or tablet ships with a charging cable matching that device's charging port (Lightning, USB-C, or micro-USB, depending on the model and its age). Beyond the charging cable, what's included varies by product line:

- **Wall power adapters** are not included with any phone or tablet, consistent with how most manufacturers have sold new devices in recent years, unless a listing explicitly states "adapter included."
- **Wired headphones** are not included with any device.
- **SIM ejector tools** are included with devices that use a physical SIM tray.
- **Styluses (Apple Pencil, S Pen)** are only included when explicitly stated on the specific product listing — many stylus-compatible devices are sold without a stylus in the box.
- **Keyboard accessories** for tablets are sold separately and are never included with a tablet purchase unless explicitly bundled and stated on the listing.
- **Cases and screen protectors** are not included with any device by default, though they're available for separate purchase in our accessories catalog and we recommend adding one, especially for Grade B and Grade C devices to help protect against further cosmetic wear.

Always check the specific product listing's "What's Included" section before purchasing, since this is the authoritative source for what ships with any individual listing — general policies described here can have exceptions for specific bundled promotions.

## Accessories Catalog

Our accessories catalog includes:

- **Charging cables and wall adapters** — sold individually or as a combo, in various lengths and wattages
- **Cases** — for popular phone and tablet models, in various styles (clear, rugged/protective, wallet-style, folio for tablets)
- **Screen protectors** — tempered glass and film options for popular models

Accessory compatibility is listed clearly on each accessory's product page, specifying exactly which device models it fits — always verify compatibility with your specific device model before purchasing an accessory, since even visually similar models (such as consecutive generations of the same product line) frequently have different dimensions, port placement, or camera cutouts that make accessories non-interchangeable.

## Charging Port Types by Device Age

Understanding charging port types helps when deciding whether existing cables and chargers will work with a newly purchased refurbished device:

- **USB-C** — the current standard across nearly all recent Android phones and tablets, and current-generation iPhones and iPads.
- **Lightning** — Apple's proprietary connector used on iPhones and some iPads before their transition to USB-C. Lightning cables and USB-C cables are not interchangeable without an adapter.
- **Micro-USB** — an older standard, found on some older or budget Android devices still in circulation. Not compatible with USB-C or Lightning cables without an adapter.

The port type for any specific device is listed in that device's product specifications — this is especially important to check if you're planning to reuse an existing cable, charger, or car mount rather than purchasing new accessories.

## Wireless Charging Compatibility

Most recent-generation phones support Qi wireless charging, which works with any Qi-certified wireless charging pad regardless of brand — a wireless charger doesn't need to be from the same manufacturer as your phone to work correctly. Apple's MagSafe system, available on recent iPhone models, adds magnetic alignment on top of standard Qi charging — a MagSafe charger will still work as a standard (non-magnetic) Qi charger with non-MagSafe phones, and non-MagSafe Qi chargers will still charge MagSafe-compatible iPhones, just without the magnetic snap-alignment feature. Whether a specific device supports wireless charging at all is listed in that device's specifications, since not every model (particularly older or budget devices) includes this feature.

## Case and Screen Protector Fit Across Generations

A very common question is whether an existing case or screen protector from a previous device will fit a newly purchased device. As a general rule, cases and screen protectors are designed for a specific model and generation and are not guaranteed to fit even the very next generation of the same product line, due to small changes in dimensions, button placement, or camera module size and position between generations. We recommend purchasing a case and screen protector specifically listed as compatible with your new device's exact model rather than assuming an existing accessory will fit.

## SIM Card Compatibility

Phones (and cellular-enabled tablets) use one of a few physical SIM card sizes — nano-SIM is the current standard across nearly all modern devices. If you're moving a SIM card from an older device, most carriers can also issue a replacement SIM in the correct size if your existing SIM doesn't match what your new device requires, or your carrier may support eSIM activation instead of a physical SIM card, depending on your device and carrier. Check your device's listing for which SIM formats and eSIM support (if any) it includes.

## International Charger and Adapter Compatibility

Devices shipped internationally may require a regional plug adapter if the included cable's charging brick (when applicable) or any separately purchased wall adapter uses a plug shape not standard in your country. USB-C and Lightning cables themselves are not region-specific — only the wall plug shape varies by country, so a simple plug adapter (not a voltage converter) is typically all that's needed, since most modern phone and tablet chargers are designed to automatically handle different input voltages worldwide.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: If I buy a case at the same time as my device, will it be shipped together?**
A: Yes, accessories purchased in the same order as a device ship together in the same package, unless an item is temporarily out of stock, in which case you'll be notified with the option to ship separately or wait for full availability.

**Q: Can I return an accessory separately from the device I bought it with?**
A: Yes, accessories follow the same 30-day return policy as devices and can be returned independently, even if the device itself is being kept — see the Returns and Refunds article for the general return process.

**Q: Do you sell official manufacturer accessories, or third-party alternatives?**
A: Our accessories catalog includes a mix of official manufacturer accessories (such as genuine Apple or Samsung cables) and quality-tested third-party alternatives, clearly labeled as such on each product listing so customers can choose based on their preference and budget.

**Q: I lost the charging cable that came with my device — can I get a replacement?**
A: Replacement charging cables for your exact device model are available for purchase through our accessories catalog at any time, independent of your original order.
