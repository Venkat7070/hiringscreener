# Payment Methods and Financing Options

This article covers how customers can pay for their order, including installment and financing options.

## Accepted Payment Methods

We accept the following payment methods at checkout:

- **Major credit and debit cards** — Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover
- **Digital wallets** — Apple Pay and Google Pay, where supported by the customer's device and browser
- **PayPal** — including PayPal Pay in 4 for customers who qualify, as a separate installment option through PayPal directly
- **Buy Now, Pay Later financing** — through our third-party financing partner, described below

We do not currently accept payment by check, money order, wire transfer, or cash, since we are an online-only retailer without a physical storefront to process these safely.

## Buy Now, Pay Later Financing

For customers who'd prefer to spread the cost of their device over time, we offer financing through a third-party lending partner, selectable at checkout. Financing options typically include:

- **Pay in 4:** Split the purchase into 4 equal payments, made every 2 weeks, interest-free if paid on schedule.
- **3, 6, or 12-month installment plans:** Longer-term financing with fixed monthly payments. Whether interest applies depends on the customer's creditworthiness and the specific plan selected during the financing application — exact terms are shown before the customer confirms the loan, and approval and specific rates are determined by our financing partner, not by ReNew Mobile directly.

Financing approval is subject to a soft credit check (which does not affect credit score) at the application stage, and in some cases a hard credit check if the customer proceeds with a longer-term plan — this is disclosed by our financing partner during the application process before it's performed.

Financing eligibility, approval, and exact terms are determined entirely by our financing partner based on their own underwriting criteria. ReNew Mobile does not have visibility into why a specific application was approved or declined, or ability to override a financing decision — if a customer has questions about their financing application status or terms, they should contact our financing partner's support directly, using the contact information provided in their financing application confirmation email.

## When You're Charged

For standard (non-financed) orders, the full order amount is charged at the time of purchase, not when the order ships. For Pay in 4 or installment financing, the first installment is typically charged at the time of purchase, with subsequent installments charged automatically according to the schedule shown at checkout.

## Sales Tax

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the shipping destination, in accordance with applicable state and local tax requirements. The exact tax amount is shown before payment is confirmed, and is included in the total charged.

## Promo Codes and Discounts

Promotional codes, where available, can be entered at checkout before completing payment. Only one promo code can typically be applied per order unless otherwise stated in the specific promotion's terms. Promo codes cannot be applied retroactively to an order that has already been placed — if you have a valid code you forgot to apply, contact support promptly after placing the order, and we'll do our best to apply it before the order ships, though this isn't always possible depending on how quickly the order has already been processed.

## Price Match and Price Adjustments

If the price of an item you purchased drops within 7 days of your order date (excluding limited-time flash sales or clearance pricing), contact support with your order number and we'll issue a refund for the price difference, provided the order hasn't yet shipped. This does not apply to trade-in credit adjustments or bundle promotions.

## Payment Failures

If a payment fails at checkout, this is most commonly due to a card issuer decline (insufficient funds, incorrect billing information, or a fraud-prevention hold placed by the card issuer), rather than an issue on our end. Customers experiencing repeated payment failures should verify their billing address matches what's on file with their card issuer exactly, try an alternate payment method, or contact their card issuer directly to ask about the decline before retrying.

## Refund Timing for Different Payment Methods

Refunds are issued back to the original payment method used. For credit/debit cards, refunds typically post within 3–7 business days after we issue them, depending on the card issuer. For PayPal, refunds are usually reflected within 1–2 business days. For financed orders, refunds are processed through our financing partner and may take slightly longer, since the partner needs to adjust or cancel the remaining installment schedule — customers should also confirm with the financing partner that any automatic installment charges scheduled after a return has been processed are correctly stopped.

## Security and Fraud Protection

All payment information is processed through PCI-compliant, encrypted payment processing — we do not store full credit card numbers on our own servers. Orders may occasionally be flagged for manual review as a fraud-prevention measure, particularly for first-time customers making a large purchase or orders shipping to an address different from the billing address. If your order is placed on hold for review, you'll typically be contacted by email or phone within 1 business day to verify the order before it's released for processing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I pay for part of my order with a gift card and the rest with a credit card?**
A: Yes, gift card balances can be applied at checkout and any remaining balance charged to a credit/debit card or other accepted payment method.

**Q: Will applying for financing affect my credit score?**
A: The initial pre-qualification check is typically a soft credit inquiry that does not affect your credit score. If you proceed with certain longer-term financing plans, a hard credit inquiry may be required, which is disclosed clearly before it's performed — check the specific terms shown during the financing application for the plan you're considering.

**Q: Can I pay off my financing plan early?**
A: This depends on the terms of the specific financing plan and is managed directly through our financing partner — check your financing agreement or contact the financing partner's support for early payoff options and whether any interest is reduced by paying early.

**Q: Do you charge my card again if the order price changes due to shipping upgrades I add later?**
A: Any changes to your order made after the original purchase, such as upgrading shipping speed, are processed as a separate incremental charge to your original payment method at the time the change is made, with authorization requested from you before the additional charge is applied.
