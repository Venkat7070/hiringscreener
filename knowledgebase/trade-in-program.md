# Trade-In Program

ReNew Mobile offers a trade-in program that lets customers exchange their old phone or tablet for credit toward a new purchase, or for direct cash payment. This article explains how the program works, how valuations are determined, and what to expect at each step.

## How Trade-In Works

1. **Get an estimated quote online.** On our trade-in page, select the brand, model, storage capacity, and answer a short series of condition questions about your device (screen condition, functional issues, battery health if known, and cosmetic condition). This generates an instant estimated quote.
2. **Ship your device to us.** If you accept the estimated quote, we provide a prepaid shipping label and box requirements (or you can use your own secure packaging) to send your device to our facility.
3. **Inspection.** Once we receive your device, our team inspects it against the condition details you provided, confirms functionality through our standard diagnostic process, and verifies the device is not reported lost, stolen, or still financed via IMEI/serial blacklist check.
4. **Final offer.** If the device matches the condition described during the online quote, your final trade-in value matches your estimated quote and is processed within 1–2 business days of inspection. If the device's actual condition differs from what was described (for example, a cracked screen that wasn't disclosed, or a non-functional camera), we'll send a revised offer reflecting the actual condition for your approval before proceeding.
5. **Payment.** You can choose to receive your trade-in value as store credit (redeemable toward any ReNew Mobile purchase, often with a bonus credit amount as an incentive) or as a direct payment to your original payment method or bank account, depending on the option selected during the trade-in process.

## What Affects Trade-In Value

- **Model and age** — newer, higher-demand models generally receive higher trade-in values than older models
- **Storage capacity** — higher storage capacity devices of the same model generally receive a higher trade-in value
- **Functional condition** — a fully functional device receives significantly more value than one with a cracked screen, non-functional camera, or other hardware issues
- **Battery health** — devices with significantly degraded battery health may receive a reduced valuation, since battery replacement may be required before the device can be resold
- **Cosmetic condition** — heavy cosmetic damage (deep scratches, dents, cracked back glass) can reduce trade-in value, though cosmetic wear generally affects value less than functional issues
- **Account lock status** — devices that arrive still locked to a previous owner's account (Activation Lock, Google FRP, or Samsung Reactivation Lock) cannot be valued or processed until the lock is removed; see "Before You Ship Your Device" below

## Devices Not Eligible for Trade-In

- Devices reported lost or stolen (checked via IMEI/serial blacklist during inspection)
- Devices still financed or leased through a carrier or third party where the customer does not yet have full ownership
- Devices with severe water damage affecting the motherboard, where repair is not economically viable
- Devices that cannot pass a basic power-on test, unless specifically accepted under a "damaged device" trade-in category, if offered — check our trade-in page for whether non-powering devices are currently accepted and at what reduced valuation category

## Before You Ship Your Device

To ensure a smooth trade-in process, before shipping your device please:

1. **Back up your data**, since your device will be data-wiped as part of our process and cannot be recovered afterward.
2. **Sign out of your account and remove all locks:**
   - **iPhone/iPad:** Go to Settings > [Your Name] > Sign Out, which removes iCloud Activation Lock. Also turn off Find My iPhone/iPad before signing out, if it wasn't already disabled by the sign-out process.
   - **Android phones/tablets:** Remove your Google account from Settings > Accounts, and for Samsung devices, also remove your Samsung account and disable Find My Mobile / Reactivation Lock in Settings > Biometrics and security > Find My Mobile.
3. **Perform a factory reset** on the device after removing accounts, to ensure your personal data is cleared before it leaves your possession — while we perform our own certified data wipe upon receipt, resetting beforehand protects your data during transit and speeds up our inspection process.
4. **Remove any case, screen protector, or SIM card** before shipping, unless a case is being traded in as part of a bundle (check your trade-in quote for whether accessories are included in the valuation).

Devices that arrive still locked to a previous account cannot be valued or processed for trade-in credit until the lock is removed. If this happens, we'll contact you with instructions to remotely remove the lock if possible, or the device may need to be returned to you unprocessed if the lock cannot be resolved remotely.

## Trade-In Toward a New Purchase

Customers trading in a device to put credit toward a new purchase have two options:

- **Trade in first, then order** — receive your trade-in credit as store credit, then use it toward a separate purchase.
- **Trade in and order together** — during checkout for a new device, you can add a trade-in to the same order; the estimated trade-in value is deducted from your order total at checkout, and the balance is confirmed once we receive and inspect your traded-in device (with a revised charge or credit if the actual condition differs from what was described).

## Timeline

- **Estimated quote:** Instant, online.
- **Shipping to us:** Typically 3–5 business days in transit, depending on your location and the shipping method selected for your prepaid label.
- **Inspection and final offer:** Typically 1–2 business days after we receive your device.
- **Payment or credit issuance:** Same day as final offer approval for store credit; 3–5 business days for direct payment to a bank account or original payment method, depending on your selected payout method.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is my trade-in quote guaranteed, or can it change?**
A: Your quote is guaranteed as long as the device matches the condition details you provided during the online quote process. If the device's actual condition differs upon inspection, we'll send a revised offer for your approval before finalizing anything — nothing changes without your confirmation.

**Q: What happens if I don't accept the revised offer after inspection?**
A: If you decline a revised offer, we'll return your device to you at no cost, typically within 3–5 business days.

**Q: Can I trade in more than one device at a time?**
A: Yes, our trade-in process supports submitting multiple devices in the same shipment — each device receives its own individual quote and inspection.

**Q: Do you accept broken or non-powering devices for trade-in?**
A: This depends on current program terms — check our trade-in page for whether a "damaged device" category is currently accepted, and if so, at what valuation tier, since this changes based on our current parts and repair needs.
