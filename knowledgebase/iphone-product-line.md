# iPhone Product Line

ReNew Mobile carries a wide selection of refurbished iPhone models. This article covers what's typically available, how iPhone-specific specs and terminology work, and common questions specific to buying a refurbished iPhone.

## Models Typically Available

Our iPhone inventory rotates based on incoming trade-in and buyback supply, but commonly includes recent-generation models spanning the last several years of releases, along with older models while supply and demand support keeping them in our catalog. Each product listing shows the exact model, storage capacity, color, and grade currently in stock — availability for any specific combination of model, storage, color, and grade varies day to day as inventory turns over.

Older iPhone models are eventually phased out of our active catalog once parts availability for repairs becomes too limited to reliably refurbish them to our quality standard, or once a model no longer receives current iOS security updates from Apple, since we prioritize selling devices that can still run supported, secure software.

## Storage Options

iPhones are listed by their storage capacity (for example, 128GB, 256GB, 512GB), which is fixed at the time of manufacture and cannot be upgraded after purchase — there is no external storage expansion (such as an SD card slot) on any iPhone model. Customers should choose their storage capacity carefully at the time of purchase based on their expected usage (photos/video, apps, offline media), since exchanging for a different storage size later requires a return and new purchase rather than an in-place upgrade.

## Carrier Status

Each listing clearly states whether a device is:

- **Unlocked** — compatible with any carrier that supports the device's cellular bands, and can be used with any SIM card without carrier restriction. Most of our inventory is unlocked.
- **Carrier-locked** — tied to a specific carrier and requires a SIM card from that carrier to activate cellular service, unless the customer separately requests an unlock from that carrier (which is outside our control once the device leaves our facility, and not guaranteed to be possible depending on the carrier's own unlock eligibility rules).

Customers switching networks or using an international SIM should choose an Unlocked listing to guarantee compatibility with their carrier of choice.

## Battery Health on iPhones

Every iPhone we sell has a battery health of at least 85% of original design capacity at time of sale, as measured through Apple's own battery health diagnostics accessible in iPhone Settings under Battery > Battery Health & Charging. Customers can verify this themselves after receiving their device by checking that menu. See our Battery Health and Device Care article for more detail on how battery health is measured and what to expect over time.

## Face ID and Touch ID

For models equipped with Face ID, our refurbishment process confirms Face ID functions correctly before the device is listed for sale — this is part of our standard diagnostic testing described in the About Us and Refurbishment Process article. For older models with Touch ID instead of Face ID, the fingerprint sensor is similarly tested for functionality. Neither Face ID nor Touch ID can be pre-configured with the previous owner's biometric data — customers set up their own Face ID or Touch ID during initial device setup, the same as they would with a brand-new iPhone.

## iCloud Activation Lock

One of the most important checks in our refurbishment process is confirming that iCloud Activation Lock has been fully removed from every iPhone before it's listed for sale. Activation Lock ties an iPhone to the previous owner's Apple ID and, if not removed, prevents the device from being set up by a new owner. Every iPhone sold by ReNew Mobile is guaranteed to be free of Activation Lock — if a customer ever encounters an Activation Lock screen during setup asking for a previous owner's Apple ID or password, this should be reported to support immediately, as it indicates an error in our process and will be resolved with an immediate replacement or refund at no cost to the customer.

## iOS Version and Software Updates

iPhones ship with the most current iOS version compatible with that model installed at the time of refurbishment. Customers can and should check for further software updates after initial setup (Settings > General > Software Update), since Apple releases updates on an ongoing basis after a device leaves our facility. We do not artificially restrict or delay software updates on any device we sell.

## eSIM Support

Recent iPhone models support eSIM (an embedded SIM built into the device that doesn't require a physical SIM card) in addition to or instead of a physical SIM card slot, depending on the model and region it was originally sold in. Whether a specific unit supports a physical SIM, eSIM, or both is listed on the product page — this can vary by the region the device was originally manufactured and sold for, so customers with a strong preference for physical SIM support should verify this on the specific listing before purchasing.

## Included Accessories

Refurbished iPhones from ReNew Mobile ship with a USB-C or Lightning charging cable (matching the port on that model), but do not include a wall power adapter or wired headphones, consistent with how Apple itself has sold new iPhones in recent years. Customers who need a wall adapter can add one from our accessories catalog at checkout.

## Common Compatibility Questions

**Will my old iPhone accessories work with a refurbished iPhone?**
This depends on the specific accessory and the port/connector used by the model purchased. Older iPhones use a Lightning port, while newer models have moved to USB-C — check the product listing's specifications for which port a specific model uses before assuming an existing cable or accessory will be compatible.

**Can I transfer my data from my old iPhone to my refurbished one?**
Yes — refurbished iPhones support the same data transfer methods as new iPhones, including Quick Start (direct transfer from a nearby iPhone), restoring from an iCloud backup, or restoring from a computer backup via Finder or iTunes.

**Will my refurbished iPhone work with my carrier's 5G network?**
This depends on both the specific iPhone model's supported cellular bands and your carrier's network — most recent-generation iPhones support 5G, but exact band compatibility can vary by which regional version of the device was originally manufactured for. If 5G compatibility with your specific carrier is important, contact support with the exact model and your carrier before ordering to confirm compatibility.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do refurbished iPhones come with a warranty from Apple?**
A: No — Apple's manufacturer warranty (AppleCare) is tied to the original purchaser and original point of sale, and does not transfer to secondhand devices. All ReNew Mobile iPhones are instead covered under our own 12-month warranty, described in the Warranty Policy article, which covers the same categories of hardware defects.

**Q: Can I purchase AppleCare+ separately for a refurbished iPhone?**
A: This depends on Apple's own eligibility rules for AppleCare+ on secondhand devices, which are set by Apple and outside our control — customers interested in this should check directly with Apple or an Apple Store regarding eligibility for the specific model and its manufacture date.

**Q: Is a refurbished iPhone eligible for Apple's trade-in program in the future?**
A: Yes, provided the device passes Apple's own trade-in inspection criteria at that time — Apple trade-in valuation is entirely determined by Apple based on the device's condition at the time of that future trade-in, not by anything related to it having been purchased as refurbished.
